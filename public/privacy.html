SwingView AI Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 29, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 29, 2026

01

Introduction

SwingView AI is operated by Predictive Growth Labs, based in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what the app collects, what leaves your phone and when, who processes it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

02

What Stays on Your Phone

Recording and pose detection happen on your iPhone. While the camera is on, the app tracks your body position on the device (using MediaPipe) to detect each swing and measure tempo. The live camera feed is not streamed to any server.

Your recordings, swing history and profile are stored on your phone. Recordings saved on your phone include sound if you allow the microphone. If you allow access to Photos, the app also saves recordings to your photo library. If you add a name to your profile, it stays on your phone and is not sent to us.

03

What Is Uploaded for AI Analysis

AI analysis and coaching run on our servers, not on your phone. The app asks for your agreement first. Until you agree, no swing video is uploaded for analysis and no chat request is made.

When you tap Analyze, the app uploads that swing clip to SwingView's servers. The clip is sent without sound. Faces in analysis uploads are not blurred.

With each analysis the app also sends:

  • A device identifier (Apple's identifier for vendor). It keeps your requests and sessions together. It is not your name, Apple ID or phone number.
  • A small profile: your total sessions and swings, recurring issues from earlier coaching, handedness, skill level, camera position and the club you selected.
04

AI Coach Chat

When you use the coach, the messages you type are sent to SwingView's servers with your device identifier, along with your golfer profile (such as skill level and goals) and any session goal you set.

Each completed swing analysis (its measurements and coaching text) is added to your chat session so the coach can refer to it.

05

Who Processes Your Data

Three outside companies process data for SwingView:

  • Cloudflare hosts our servers and storage. Everything the app uploads passes through or is stored on Cloudflare: swing videos, chat conversations, profile details, the device identifier and your IP address (which any server sees when the app connects, and which we use to limit abuse).
  • Modal runs our swing analysis service. It downloads the uploaded clip and measures it, and receives the profile and club sent with the analysis.
  • Anthropic provides Claude, the AI model that writes coaching text and chat replies. Anthropic receives your swing measurements and analysis text, your golfer profile, the chat messages you type and recent conversation history.

These companies process this data to provide the service. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

06

What We Store and for How Long

  • Chat sessions, messages, the swing analyses added to them and the golfer profile sent with them are stored on SwingView's servers for up to 90 days, linked to your device identifier.
  • Videos uploaded for analysis are stored on SwingView's servers. You can ask us to delete them.
  • Clips contributed for training, if you opt in, are stored on SwingView's servers. You can ask us to delete them.
  • Data travels between the app and our servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Recordings and history on your phone stay there until you delete them or delete the app. Copies saved to Photos stay in your photo library until you delete them there.
07

Optional Training Contribution

Separately from AI analysis, you can choose to contribute swing clips to help improve SwingView's swing analysis. This is optional and stays off unless you opt in. Analysis uploads are not part of it, and AI analysis and the coach work the same whether or not you contribute.

Contributed clips are sent without sound. They are face-blurred on your phone before upload, using Apple's Vision framework. Blurring is best effort: a frame where no face is detected is sent as recorded, so a face may sometimes be visible.

Each contributed clip is uploaded with pose landmarks, swing phases, tempo, skill level and camera position, plus recording details (device model, iOS version, app version, video resolution and the capture time). No name or email is attached.

You can turn contribution off at any time in Settings > Data & privacy. Clips waiting to upload are removed and uploads in progress are stopped. Clips that already reached our servers stay there until you request deletion.

08

No Accounts, Ads or Tracking

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your email address. A name you add to your profile stays on your phone. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising and no tracking. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.

09

Turning AI Processing Off

You can turn AI processing off at any time: open Settings from the gear on the Me tab and switch off "AI analysis & coaching" under Data & privacy. While it is off, no swing video is uploaded for analysis and no chat request is made, so AI analysis and the coach are unavailable.

Turning it off stops further uploads. It does not delete what was already sent. To remove that, request deletion.

10

Deleting Your Data

On your phone: swipe a swing in the Sessions tab to delete it, or delete the app to remove everything the app stored on the device. Copies saved to Photos are deleted in the Photos app.

On our servers: to delete clips you contributed for training, go to Settings > Data & privacy > Manage my data and tap Request data deletion. To delete videos uploaded for analysis and your coach conversations, email support@swingview.ai. Deletion requests are handled within 30 days.

11

The SwingView Website

This section applies to swingview.ai, not to the app. If you join the waitlist on the website, we receive the email address you enter. The form is handled by Formspree, which receives that address to deliver it to us. You can ask us to remove it at any time by emailing support@swingview.ai.

12

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent you have given. To use any of these rights, contact us at support@swingview.ai.

13

Children

SwingView is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us data through the app, contact us and we will delete it.
